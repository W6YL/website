# Tuesday W6YL UHF Net

W6YL holds an amateur radio net every Tuesday at **7 p.m.** on the W6YL
repeater.

A radio net is a scheduled on-air gathering where amateur radio operators
check in and communicate as a group.

Club members and other licensed amateur radio operators are welcome to check in.

New to amateur radio? Come to a club meeting and we can show you how a net works.
