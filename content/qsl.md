# QSL Information

QSL cards are used by amateur radio operators to confirm radio contacts.

## Mailing Address

Please address QSL cards to:

W6YL — SJSU Amateur Radio Club  
Electrical Engineering Department  
One Washington Square  
San José, CA 95192-0084  
USA

## QSL Policy

We will try to send a QSL card for contacts when club resources permit.

We generally do not send QSL cards for FT8 contacts unless one is requested.
