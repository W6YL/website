# SJSU Amateur Radio Club — W6YL

## Learn. Build. Experiment. Get on the air.

The San José State University Amateur Radio Club brings together students
interested in radio, electronics, communications, and hands-on technology.

Whether you are a licensed amateur radio operator or have never used a radio
before, you are welcome to get involved.

**No amateur radio license or previous experience is required to join.**

### Join Us

**Club meetings:** Tuesdays, 4:30–5:30 p.m.  
**Location:** Engineering Building, Room 376A  
**Questions or want to visit the station?** Contact us through Discord or email
arc@w6yl.org.

### What Can You Do at W6YL?

- Learn how amateur radio works
- Get on the air with experienced club members
- Prepare for an FCC amateur radio license
- Experiment with antennas, radios and electronics
- Learn about repeaters and digital communications
- Participate in amateur radio nets and contests
- Meet students who share an interest in radio and technology
