# Projects and Activities

W6YL gives students opportunities to learn amateur radio through hands-on
projects and on-air activities.

Activities may include:

- Operating the W6YL amateur radio station
- Antenna projects and testing
- Radio programming and configuration
- Amateur radio contests
- Weekly radio nets
- Licensing and operating practice
- Digital communications
- Station and repeater maintenance

## Current Projects

Check back for updates on current club projects, or join us at a Tuesday
